Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This policy explains how Useful Tech Manager may process information when its creator content-management and TikTok integration features are available.

What this service is for

Useful Tech Manager is an independent service for preparing, reviewing, analyzing, and publishing creator-approved short-form technology content to TikTok. TikTok does not own, operate, sponsor, or endorse this service.

Information we may process

To provide the service, we may process information that a user authorizes or supplies, including:

  • TikTok profile information authorized by the user;
  • TikTok account statistics and analytics data;
  • Public video information available through authorized TikTok access;
  • TikTok authorization tokens required for API access;
  • Content files, captions, and publishing details prepared for publishing; and
  • Internal workflow, review, and approval data.

How access and data are handled

  • TikTok access is requested only through TikTok OAuth. We never collect TikTok passwords.
  • Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored securely on the service owner’s private infrastructure.
  • Information is used only to provide content-management, review, publishing, and analytics functionality.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not use it for unrelated advertising.
  • We do not claim certifications or compliance programs that we do not have.

Your choices

You can revoke TikTok access through TikTok. You can also request deletion of data stored by Useful Tech Manager by contacting us. Revoking access may limit the service features that depend on TikTok authorization.

Contact

For privacy or data-deletion requests, email useful.tech.ar@gmail.com.
